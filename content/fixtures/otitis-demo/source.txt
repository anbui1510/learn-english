Acute otitis media is a common childhood infection of the middle ear.
Children often present with ear pain, fever, and irritability.
Tympanic membrane examination may show bulging and reduced mobility.
Many episodes resolve without antibiotics within a few days.
Antibiotics are considered when symptoms are severe or prolonged.
Amoxicillin remains a first-line option for uncomplicated cases.
Parents should be counselled on pain control and when to seek review.
Persistent effusion after infection can affect hearing temporarily.
Follow-up is important if fever or pain continues beyond 48–72 hours.
Routine pneumatic otoscopy helps distinguish acute otitis from otitis externa.
